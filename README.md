# Internship Toolkit

A free tool for internship hunting. It checks your resume against a job posting, tracks your applications, and drafts follow-up emails.

**Live site:** https://shailveekishore-cell.github.io/internship-toolkit/

## Features
- Resume scanner: compares your resume to a posting and suggests rewrites
- Application tracker: board with stages, deadlines, and follow-up dates
- Email drafts: thank-you, networking, and follow-up messages

## Privacy
Everything runs in your browser. Your resume and applications are saved only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

## Built with
HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (no frameworks).
